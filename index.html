Haltewekker

Fall asleep on the bus or train. It wakes you before your stop.

Before you nap
  • Open this page in Safari and leave it on screen. If you lock the phone or switch apps, it can’t follow you.
  • Turn the volume up or wear earbuds. A web page can’t make an iPhone vibrate, so the sound is what wakes you.
  • Switch off Low Power Mode and plug in if you can. Turn brightness down in Control Center; the sleep screen is dark anyway.
  • Run the test alarm once, in Settings.
