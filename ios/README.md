# Haltewekker for iPhone

The iPhone app follows you with GPS while your phone is locked, rings over
your music before your stop, and shows your trip (distance, expected arrival
home, countdown) on the lock screen and in the Dynamic Island.

GitHub builds it automatically. The newest version is always here:
https://github.com/kupperma/Halte-wekker/releases/tag/latest-ios

## Installing with Sideloadly (Windows)

1. Install **iTunes** and **iCloud** from apple.com (not the Microsoft Store versions).
2. Install **Sideloadly** from sideloadly.io.
3. Download **Haltewekker.ipa** from the release page above.
4. Connect your iPhone with a cable, unlock it and tap **Trust**.
5. Open Sideloadly, drag the .ipa in, type your Apple ID, press **Start**.
6. On the iPhone: **Settings → General → VPN & Device Management** → tap your Apple ID → **Trust**.
7. Turn on **Settings → Privacy & Security → Developer Mode** (the phone restarts).
8. Open Haltewekker and allow location, notifications and Live Activities.

With a free Apple ID the app works for 7 days. Run Sideloadly again to renew it
(your settings stay).
